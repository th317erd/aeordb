## P2c Preflight Test Protocol

1. **Registry authority:** decode the checked-in binary through the production reader while using the independently generated manifest as the policy oracle. Prove all 46 families, 61 descriptors, every typed policy value, all five hash-specific cached registry instances, and admission/runtime pointer identity. The production serializer or decoder may not generate its own expected values.
2. **Path classification:** prove ordinary paths, all matcher kinds, exact known families, unknown protected paths, and runtime-only structural ancestors such as `/.aeordb-system`, `/.aeordb-system/cluster`, and `/.aeordb-config`. Structural containers are traversal-only and never inherit or invent a persisted family policy.
3. **Operation policy:** table-drive logical backup, peer replication, client sync, import, generic data, indexing, verify, repair, migration, spill, and GC decisions over every family. Pin the required corrections for permissions, conflicts, controls, credentials/secrets, derived indexes, and logs.
4. **Traversal states:** independently reproduce a valid flat directory, malformed flat root, valid B-tree, malformed inline B-tree root, missing/corrupt child branch, cycle, warning-cap exhaustion, operational I/O/admission failure, cancellation, and visitor-requested early stop. Integrity is exactly `complete`, `diagnostically_partial`, or `corrupt`; visitor completion is a separate field.
5. **Consumer behavior:** destructive or authoritative backup, import, sync, reindex, GC, counters, repair conclusions, and system-store enumeration must reject incomplete traversal. Verify and explicitly diagnostic tooling may retain readable entries while recording bounded evidence. No caller may turn partial/corrupt into an empty or complete collection.
6. **Architecture removal:** fail while legacy policy predicates or protected path arrays remain outside the named v0 byte-layout evaluator. Permit route/SDK compatibility parameters only as adapters into registry operation policy, never as lower-level authority.
7. **Verification order:** focused red target; adjacent 194-test characterization set; route/plugin/transfer/index regressions; contract and architecture gates; workspace suite; native checks; then a live home-backed disposable server exercising authorized and concealed paths plus injected traversal damage. Every command uses four Cargo jobs/threads, the home-backed test temporary directory, and a hard timeout.
